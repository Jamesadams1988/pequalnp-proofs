# Upstream bounds for unary PHP(5,4)

L-pcx-169 asks for an ordinary resolution refutation with at most 149 lines. The 20 variables say whether each of five pigeons occupies each of four holes. Five clauses require a hole for each pigeon. Forty binary clauses forbid two pigeons from sharing a hole. The public graph listed both the positive row and its negative twin as conjectures at the 22:50 UTC intake on 25 November 2026. [Positive row](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-pcx-169.4de6d1e40764.json) · [Negative twin](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-pcx-170.bca3931b5f5d.json)

**Lower bound.** rsmith25’s merged L-pcx-160 proof contains an auxiliary theorem establishing at least **101 lines** for every ordinary refutation. The named outside statement excludes proofs with at most 99 lines. [Pull request 15574](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/15574)

**Upper bound.** willakone’s merged L-pcx-171 proof gives **157 lines**, even though the named theorem allows 159. Its `cert` contains 45 distinct source axioms and 112 ordinary resolutions. Independent replay checked both parents, the complementary pivot, and the exact union after erasure for each resolution. Every line is a dependency of the final empty clause. The ordinary L-pcx-173 donor repeats the same explicit certificate. BastianMIllan’s closed versions for both rows also have 157 live lines. [Merged 171](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/13435) · [Merged 173](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/13433) · [Closed 171](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/13522) · [Closed 173](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/13543)

The L-pcx-183 and L-pcx-185 linear-resolution certificates on this same formula use singleton forms throughout. They replay as ordinary resolution in 157 lines as well. The 138-line L-pcx-199 proof uses conjunctions of two literals and cuts on those conjunctions in Res(2). MrMaleficus’s merged L-meta-9250 proof exhibits 20 learned clauses under a stated cap of 24, with reverse unit propagation validating each addition against the available formula. Those last two constructions require translation before their lengths can be compared with L-pcx-169.
