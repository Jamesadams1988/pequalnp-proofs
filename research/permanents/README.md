# Exact permanent calculations

Research by James Adams (Jamesadams1988). This folder collects reproducible calculations for three geometric complexity theory problems from the P vs NP project.

| Problem | Result | How it was checked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L-gct-109 | The generic doubly centered 3-by-3 Fourier matrix gives the stated quadratic and cubic coefficients. A nonzero line preserves the permanent, and changing one sign produces the cubic term 12z³. | Symbolic polynomial division in SymPy. The dimension bound has a [separate upstream proof](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/26). |
| L-gct-194 | The rectangular permanent is 120 for every complex pair c, d. Changing the first coefficient in the fifth row from 1 to 2 gives 120 + 24d. | The Lean file proves the original identity. A separate Python program checks all nine possible coefficients in both cases. [Exact statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-gct-194.8a807b0c69b5.json) |
| L-gct-199 | Of the 792 five-dimensional spaces spanned by five of the 12 centered Fourier modes, 192 survive the quadratic condition, four also survive the cubic, and none survive the quartic. The existence of a general five-dimensional witness remains open in the cited record. | Two independent exact enumerations, including a direct finite-field check on a quartic obstruction. [Exact statement](https://data.pequalnp.com/data/lemma/L-gct-199.228457a88649.json) |

## Why the complex dimension in L-gct-109 is one

Let ω be a primitive cube root of unity, let u = (1, ω, ω²) and v = (1, ω², ω), and regard both as column vectors. Every doubly centered 3-by-3 matrix has unique coordinates T = a uuᵀ + b uvᵀ + c vuᵀ + d vvᵀ. The coefficient check expands the permanent of J + zT, with J the all-ones matrix, to 6 + 9(ad + bc)z² + 6(a³ + b³ + c³ + d³)z³. Both nonconstant coefficients vanish on any complex linear space preserving the permanent.

Write X as the matrix with rows (a, b) and (−c, d). Its determinant is ad + bc. In any two-dimensional plane of singular matrices, two independent generators have rank one and share either a left or a right factor. Indeed, if the two left factors and the two right factors each formed a basis, the sum of the generators would be invertible.

For a common left factor p, the rows of X are p₁(x, y) and p₂(x, y), with x and y arbitrary. Dividing the cubic coefficient by six gives (p₁³ − p₂³)x³ + (p₁³ + p₂³)y³. Its vanishing would force p = 0. For a common right factor r, the rows are x(r₁, r₂) and y(r₁, r₂). The corresponding cubic is (r₁³ + r₂³)x³ + (−r₁³ + r₂³)y³, forcing r = 0. This excludes every such plane. The nonzero line generated by u(u − v)ᵀ attains dimension one. Over the reals, the quadratic coefficient equals half the sum of the squared entries of T, so the only real level space is zero.

This Fourier argument gives an independent derivation. The coordinate comparison script verifies that its two polynomials agree with the entry-coordinate polynomials in the previously merged Lean proof and detects a deliberate change to one coefficient. [Previously merged proof](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/pull/26)

## Reproduce the calculations

From this directory, run the commands below with Python 3.8 or newer. The first two scripts were tested with SymPy 1.14. The others use Python's standard library.

```sh
python3 check_dc3.py
python3 check_l_gct_109_coordinates.py
python3 check_l_gct_194_dp.py
python3 check_l_gct_199_fourier.py
python3 check_l_gct_199_fourier_review.py
```

From the repository's prepared `lean/` directory, run `lake env lean ../research/permanents/L_gct_194.lean`. The same file passed the project's full checker locally on Lean 4.36.0 at commit `511584e507325b6b1045bf75ea4a7ca116abc95e`. Its SHA-256 is `47c0dd9127785bf0f0d3e40b4a5bc7e96f94454b693bc270c2edefa88db8a54e`. The finite embedding enumeration adapts the upstream L-gct-208 proof. [Enumeration source](https://github.com/open-goat/pequalnp-proofs/blob/ba6e7a0fee8794b0adde64b6e3ec1a458d798b98/lean/PEqualNP/Outside/L_gct_208.lean#L46-L87)
